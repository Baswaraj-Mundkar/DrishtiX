Rule-Based Claim Screening (DrishtiX)

Screens for sensational wording, manipulation patterns, and source signals. It does not establish whether a claim is true or false.

Important: this is an educational heuristic tool, not a professional fact-checking service. A score is not a probability or proof. Verify claims using original sources and independent fact-checkers.
